#+TITLE: Sequential Thinking Domain Context
#+DATE: 2026-09-04

* Sequential Thinking

A dynamic, reflective, and non-linear step-by-step problem-solving framework that allows an agent to iteratively formulate hypotheses, revise prior deductions, and branch into alternative exploration paths before concluding.

* Language

** Thought
A discrete reasoning step containing analytical reflection, hypothesis generation, verification, or conclusion.
_Avoid_: Step, message, turn, prompt, log

** Thought Number
A 1-based integer position tracking the global chronological sequence of thoughts within a session.
_Avoid_: Index, step ID, 0-based index

** Total Thoughts
The dynamic estimate of total thought steps needed to resolve a problem.
_Avoid_: Max thoughts, limit, thought budget

** Revision
An explicit correction, amendment, or reconsideration of a previously recorded thought in history.
_Avoid_: Overwrite, edit, undo, rollback

** Branch
A named line of exploration diverging from a specific historical thought origin.
_Avoid_: Fork, thread, child session, sub-task

** Thought History
The linear, chronological record of all processed thoughts across a thinking session.
_Avoid_: Context buffer, chat history, conversation state

** Branch History
The collection of thoughts associated with a specific branch label.
_Avoid_: Branch stack, sub-thread

** Task Counter
A lightweight, non-reflective monotonic sequence tracker for iterative multi-turn tasks.
_Avoid_: Thought, reflection, hypothesis, timer
